# Roadmap

## Next

- Nothing planned yet. Ideas welcome from staff once they've used it.

## 24 November 2026: sorting, and last seen

- The list under the chart is sorted like the chart: most recent post first. In the RP view, members without an RP post in the period are sorted by their last RP post, then by their last post of any kind, then by when they were last seen. "Fewest days left first" turns the order around, and "Name" sorts it by name.
- New sort, **Last seen first**, for both lists. They stay split into members who posted and members who didn't.
- Every row says when the member was last seen.
- In the RP view, a member who never made an RP post shows the date of their last post of any kind.
- RP categories now include subcategories at any depth, not only the first level.

## 24 November 2026: first version

- Data Explorer query **Member activity**: everyone who posted or was seen in a period, with their last post overall and in the RP categories, and their post counts. Shared with staff.
- Horizontal bar chart drawn in any post with `[wrap=member-activity][/wrap]`, for staff only: avatar and name, a bar counting down the days left in the period, and the days left and post count beside it.
- Period from 1 to 24 months (3 by default, 90 days), with − and +.
- Three views: all posts, RP posts, both.
- Colours: Eurth blue, yellow at 30 days left, orange at 14, red at 7, with guide lines where they change.
- Members seen in the period without posting, with black bars growing left for how far past the limit they are.
- Sorting by most days left, fewest days left or name.

## Not planned

- **A public page.** The chart is for staff reviewing activity; members' last-seen times aren't public.
- **A separate Eurth tool or site.** It belongs on the forum, next to the staff who use it.
- **Chat messages as activity.** Activity here means posting on the forum.
